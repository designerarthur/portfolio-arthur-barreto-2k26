01 · About

Built by Digio, for Uber's partners

Uber Conta is a digital account made to help the drivers and couriers who partner with Uber receive and manage their earnings. Payments arrive right away, and partners can transfer money, pay bills and take out loans.

The product

Earnings land in the account and stay useful

After each trip the money arrives in the app. From the home screen, drivers see their balance and reach what they do most: pay bills, send a Pix or a transfer, and top up a phone, without leaving Uber Conta.

03 · Process

From question to prototype

Research framed the question, design made it testable, and a navigable prototype let people try the product before anything was built.

Research

Learn whether drivers would hire Crédito Pessoal and how tailored credit opens new revenue.

My role

Development

A prototype that walks the driver through the whole journey, from the first offer to hiring the product.

The prototype

The whole loan journey, from first offer to hiring

The prototype starts on the Uber Conta home, where Crédito Pessoal sits next to the balance. The driver picks an amount, compares installments, reviews and accepts the terms until the request goes to analysis. People could try the product before anything was built.

Next chapterDesign foundation

Design foundation

Built on Base, Uber's design system

Crédito Pessoal lives inside the Uber ecosystem, so it follows Base, Uber's design system, instead of a visual language of its own. Base gives every product shared foundations, and this film introduces them: motion (Move), accessibility and layout. The result is a new product that feels like Uber from the first screen.

04 · Results

What happened when it shipped

Design took 1 to 4 weeks, about one month of work. To measure its effect, we ran an MVP for three months. In the acquisition phase we recorded 1,500 subscriptions and 0% detractors, a high level of user satisfaction.
